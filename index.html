Explore Dholera

A city being planned at a different scale.

Roughly 65 km from Ahmedabad, Dholera is being developed as a Special Investment Region under the Delhi–Mumbai Industrial Corridor — a proposed 21,387-hectare smart city tied into a new expressway, an international airport, and the west-coast freight corridor.

01 · Operational

On the ground, working today

The 109 km Ahmedabad–Dholera Expressway (NE-8) is operational, alongside the 300 MW Solar Park and full underground smart utilities active in the activation zone.

02 · Under construction

Cranes up, foundations set

Dholera International Airport with its 3,200 m Code 4E runway is advancing toward operational readiness, alongside the dedicated semiconductor and high-tech manufacturing cluster.

03 · Sanctioned

Drawn, sanctioned & expanding

Approved semi-high-speed transit connecting Ahmedabad directly to Dholera SIR, setting the stage for continuous multi-phase expansion across the 21,387-hectare smart region.

Dholera Special Investment Region (SIR) is a planned greenfield industrial development in Gujarat, India. The project aims to support industrial growth and planned infrastructure development. Development and infrastructure status can vary across different areas, so buyers should verify the specific property's location and current conditions.

Dholera may interest buyers looking at long-term land opportunities because of its planned industrial development and evolving connectivity. However, land prices and future returns are not guaranteed. Before investing, evaluate the purchase price, exact location, land-use permissions, title documents, infrastructure availability and resale risks. Consider independent legal and financial advice.

Residential plot prices in Dholera vary according to location, plot size, road access, development status, permitted land use and documentation. Contact our team for current availability and property-specific pricing. Always compare the total cost, including applicable charges, and verify the exact plot details before making a booking.

Before making a decision, check current updates from official government sources and distinguish between operational facilities, projects under construction and proposed developments.
